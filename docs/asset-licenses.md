# Asset licences

Every third-party image on this site is recorded here with its licence.
Nothing ships without an entry.

## Service showcase (WI-1 / WI-2) — OUTSTANDING

The six slides carry a fully built image card: 4:5 portrait, hairline
border, two-layer shadow, fixed mask with the picture easing 1.08 → 1.0 as
its slide centres, and the shared grade (desaturated to ≤15% chroma,
graphite-and-silver duotone on the site tokens, grain, blacks crushed to
`--bg-deep`, slide hue into the shadows at ≤8%). **Only the photographs are
missing.** Each slot names the subject it is waiting for.

| 06 | Two towers / joined structures, abstract-graphic | TODO(asset) |

**Why nothing was shipped in the interim slot.** WI-2 permits licensed
editorial photography as an interim state, but a licence has to be *held*
and recorded here — and this build has no licensed source to draw from.
Pulling images off the web would put unlicensed work into a commercial
site and fabricate the provenance this file exists to record. The repo's
own photography does not cover it either: of seven files, three are stock
people (banned by the art direction), one is the logo, and
`consulting.jpg` / `law.jpg` are already logged in §15 as placeholder
stock awaiting replacement.

So the slots render as marked placeholders — visibly `TODO(asset)`, in the
final grade, at the final dimensions. Dropping in real exports is a data
change, not a rebuild.

**Art direction for whoever sources them:** architectural and material
subjects only. No faces, no handshakes, no clip-art metaphors, no
AI-generated imagery, no landmark clichés. Exact-size exports, AVIF with
WebP fallback, ≤200KB each, `alt` naming the subject, and the licence
recorded in the table above.
